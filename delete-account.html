# Habit Battle – Account Deletion ## How to Request Account Deletion Users of Habit Battle can request the deletion of their account and associated personal data. To request deletion: 1. Send an email to **[YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]**. 2. Use the subject line: **Habit Battle Account Deletion Request**. 3. Include the email address registered with your Habit Battle account. 4. Our support team will verify your request and process it. ## Data That Will Be Deleted After a verified deletion request is processed, we intend to delete: - Your Habit Battle account and profile information. - Your saved habits and habit progress. - Your streaks and challenge participation records. - Your friend relationships and associated social data. - Other personal information associated with your account, where applicable. ## Data That May Be Retained Certain information may be retained when required by applicable law, for security, fraud prevention, or legitimate operational purposes.
