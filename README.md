# Privacy Policy – Habit Battle

**Effective Date:** November 13, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 13, 2026

Habit Battle ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Habit Battle collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

By using Habit Battle, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect the following information:

### Account Information
- Name and profile information you provide.
- Email address and account authentication information.
- Other information you voluntarily add to your profile.

### Habit and Activity Information
- Habits you create and manage.
- Daily check-ins and habit completion records.
- Progress, streaks, and challenge-related information.
- Other activity associated with your use of Habit Battle.

### Friends and Social Features
- Friend requests and friendship relationships.
- Information necessary to operate social and challenge features.
- Interactions with other users within the application.

### Device and Notification Information
If you enable push notifications, we may process device notification tokens and related information required to deliver notifications to your device.

Technical information such as device and application details may also be processed to maintain application functionality and security.

## 2. Contacts Permission

Habit Battle may request permission to access your device contacts to help you find and invite friends.

**Your contacts remain on your device.**

- Contacts are accessed locally on your device.
- Contact names and phone numbers are not uploaded to our backend servers.
- We do not store your contact list on our servers.
- You can deny or revoke contacts permission through your device settings.

Granting contacts permission is optional. You can continue using other application features without granting it.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage your account.
- Authenticate users and protect account access.
- Save and display your habits and progress.
- Provide friend, invitation, and challenge features.
- Deliver notifications when enabled.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve application functionality.
- Detect misuse and protect the security of our services.
- Respond to support requests.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Information required for application functionality is processed through our backend services and database.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate Habit Battle, such as backend hosting, database infrastructure, and notification delivery providers.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the rights, security, and safety of our users and services.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account and activity information for as long as necessary to provide Habit Battle and fulfill the purposes described in this policy.

If you want your account and associated personal information deleted, contact us using the email address provided below.

We will review and process deletion requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

## 7. Your Choices and Permissions

You can:

- Choose whether to grant contacts permission.
- Enable or disable notifications through your device settings.
- Choose what profile and habit information you provide.
- Request deletion of your account and associated personal information.

Revoking a device permission does not automatically delete information already associated with your account.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Habit Battle is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws
